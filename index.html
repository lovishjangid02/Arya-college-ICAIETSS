Author Guidelines

Paper Submission Guidelines

Authors are cordially invited to submit original, unpublished research papers demonstrating technical novelty and empirical validation.

Paper Length & Template

Papers must strictly follow the standard IEEE / Springer double-column conference format.

  • Length: 6 to 8 full pages (inclusive of references)
  • Extra pages: Max 2 pages allowed with supplementary charge
  • Format: PDF submission only via portal

Plagiarism & Ethics

All submissions undergo mandatory similarity screening via Turnitin / CrossCheck prior to peer review.

  • Similarity Index must be strictly below 15%
  • No single source similarity exceeding 4%
  • Self-plagiarism and dual submission strictly prohibited

Double-Blind Review

ICETIS-2026 follows an uncompromising double-blind peer review methodology.

  • Omit author names, affiliations, and emails in initial PDF
  • Every paper evaluated by at least 2 independent experts
  • Review criteria: novelty, methodology, rigor, readability
