Welcome to ICAIETSS-2027

Fostering Breakthroughs in Technology & Innovation

A premier global forum connecting scientists, academicians, industry visionaries, and researchers to deliberate on next-generation computing, intelligent autonomous systems, and sustainable technological solutions.

Pioneering Interdisciplinary Research in the Pink City

The 1st International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technology and Smart Systems (ICAIETSS-2027) serves as an intellectual catalyst addressing crucial paradigm shifts in computational intelligence, cybersecurity, smart cyber-physical systems, and green technologies.

Organized in hybrid mode (both in-person physical sessions and virtual participation for overseas scholars), the conference offers inspiring keynote addresses, rigorous peer-reviewed technical paper presentations, hands-on workshops, and interactive panel sessions.

Author Guidelines

Paper Submission Guidelines

Authors are cordially invited to submit original, unpublished research papers demonstrating technical novelty and empirical validation.

Paper Length & Template

Papers must strictly follow the standard IEEE / Springer double-column conference format.

  • Length: 6 to 8 full pages (inclusive of references)
  • Extra pages: Max 2 pages allowed with supplementary charge
  • Format: PDF submission only via portal

Plagiarism & Ethics

All submissions undergo mandatory similarity screening via Turnitin / CrossCheck prior to peer review.

  • Similarity Index must be strictly below 15%
  • No single source similarity exceeding 4%
  • Self-plagiarism and dual submission strictly prohibited

Double-Blind Review

ICAIETSS-2027 follows an uncompromising double-blind peer review methodology.

  • Omit author names, affiliations, and emails in initial PDF
  • Every paper evaluated by at least 2 independent experts
  • Review criteria: novelty, methodology, rigor, readability
